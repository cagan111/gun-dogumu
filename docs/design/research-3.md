## 1. Timely
- **Positioning/tone:** Booking + payments for salons and spas, 14-day trial, no free tier [S: softwareadvice.com/salon/timely-profile/]. Friendly, modern, "calm software" tone [I].
- **Hero:** Headline + CTA + product screenshot/device mock [I].
- **Product display:** Customisable calendar with day/week/month views; services colour-coded [S: medesk.net/en/blog/timely-review].
- **Type/color/imagery/rhythm:** Rounded geometric sans, soft pastel/teal accents, lifestyle salon photography, alternating text/screenshot bands [I].
- **Booking flow:** Online client self-booking [S]; details unverified.
- **Dashboard:** Colour-per-service blocks. Reviewers call the UI "familiar", simple [S: capterra.com/p/142756/Timely/reviews/]. Weakness cited: no custom tags to mark special appointments [S: medesk].
- **Trust:** Capterra ~4.0/5 [S]; "trusted by X salons" counters [I].
- **Premium vs generic:** Clean but generic-SaaS [I].
- **Brilliant:** Colour-as-meaning calendar (service = colour) is instantly scannable.
- **Weakness:** No flexible tagging/annotation on appointments.

## 2. Phorest
- **Positioning/tone:** Growth-and-retention platform for salons; revenue-led ("claim back time", "business planning & growth") [S: phorest.com/benefits/claim-back-time/]. Confident, ROI-heavy; stats are vendor claims [S caveat].
- **Hero:** Benefit headline, demo CTA, salon-owner photo + UI overlay [I].
- **Product display:** Branded client app (iOS/Android) per salon; PhorestGo owner app with bookings, revenue, staff performance; "health check" dashboard [S: phorest.com/features/salon-scheduling-software; getapp listing].
- **Type/color/imagery:** Bold sans, saturated brand colour with dark panels, real stylist portraits [I].
- **Booking flow:** App bookings secured by card, optional no-show fee, minimum notice/cancel windows, staff/service gating [S].
- **Dashboard:** KPI-first, numbers over timeline [S/I].
- **Trust:** Customer counts, case-study stats, awards [I].
- **Premium vs generic:** Mid-premium, corporate.
- **Brilliant:** Salon-branded app: the client sees the salon, not the vendor.
- **Weakness:** Marketing is dense with unverified uplift numbers; pricing opaque [S].

## 3. Setmore
- **Positioning/tone:** Free-tier, small-business, "get started now" friendliness [S: toolradar.com/tools/setmore, free tier up to 4 users].
- **Hero:** Short promise, sign-up CTA, booking-page mockup [I].
- **Product display:** Shareable booking page, QR code, team calendar, Google/Microsoft sync [S: pabau.com/blog/setmore-review/; g2 captions].
- **Type/color:** Friendly sans, blue/green primary, stock-photo people [I].
- **Booking flow:** Service -> provider -> date/time -> details [S: support.setmore.com/pt/articles/12570746]. Four short steps, first-available option, custom terms and redirect [S: smbguide.com/?p=5688].
- **Dashboard:** Standard grid calendar; one calendar per business, not per service [S: g2 reviews]; calendar-sync flakiness reported [S: checkthat.ai].
- **Trust:** 4,000+ reviews aggregator [S].
- **Premium vs generic:** Generic utility.
- **Brilliant:** Frictionless 4-step flow that is testable in a private window; QR code bridges physical shop to digital.
- **Weakness:** Business hours shown but outside-hours booking possible [S: smbguide]; sync reliability.

## 4. SimplyBook.me
- **Positioning/tone:** Flexible "build your booking website" toolkit for any service industry [S: simplybook.me/en/features].
- **Hero:** Industry-picker + trial CTA [I].
- **Product display:** Live preview pane in design settings, click through as a customer [S: news.simplybook.me design settings post]; showcase blog of "beautiful booking websites" [S].
- **Type/color/imagery:** Template-driven; TechRadar notes limited template variety, a top user complaint [S: techradar.com/reviews/simplybook]. Custom domain costs extra [S].
- **Booking flow:** Booking site, Facebook/Instagram, own website embed, payment at confirmation [S].
- **Dashboard:** Feature-dense admin (plugins) [I].
- **Trust:** 4.5 (1,690 reviews), 92% recommend [S: endorsal listing, vendor-adjacent].
- **Premium vs generic:** Generic by default; premium only if user customises.
- **Brilliant:** Customer-eye live preview while styling.
- **Weakness:** Template sameness; customisation pushes work to the owner.

## 5. Mindbody / Booker
- **Positioning/tone:** All-in-one for larger wellness/salon operators; "Get a demo" led, sales-driven [S: booker.com]. Corporate, feature-list voice.
- **Hero:** "All-in-one software" + demo CTA [S].
- **Product display:** Marketplace (shared Mindbody client marketplace) and email builder as selling points [S: thesalonbusiness.com/booker-vs-mindbody]; calendar actions inline (cancel, confirm) [S: Mindbody blog].
- **Design:** Described as outdated vs Boulevard/Mangomint [S: thesalonbusiness.com]. Approachable for salons [S].
- **Type/color/imagery:** Brand-blue/orange palette, stock fitness/spa photography [I].
- **Dashboard:** Dense, many-menu [I].
- **Trust:** Scale and marketplace reach.
- **Premium vs generic:** Generic-corporate.
- **Brilliant:** Consumer marketplace means demand arrives with the software.
- **Weakness:** Dated, heavy UI; cluttered.

## 6. Non-SaaS inspiration (from search listings only)
- Maison Mastrorelli v2 (French Riviera guesthouse, Nuxt, custom serif "Sfizia", immersive media): Mediterranean heritage + web craft [S: awwwards.com/sites/maison-mastrorelli-v2].
- Das James: editorial, restraint [S: designrush.com/best-designs/websites/das-james-hotel-website-design]. Dimora Palanca Firenze: large imagery, fine line/geometric details [S: designrush .../dimora-palanca-firenze-website-design].
- Pattern across them [I]: big serif display, small-caps/tracked labels, generous whitespace, warm off-white paper, few colours, slow fades.

## 7. Cross-product: clichés to AVOID
1. Purple/blue gradient hero + floating dashboard screenshot.
2. Stock photos of smiling stylists with hair-flip.
3. Pastel blobs, 3D-isometric icons, emoji bullet lists.
4. "Trusted by 10,000+" logo walls as the only trust device.
5. Pure-white, grey-border-card dashboards; default Inter/Poppins everywhere.
6. Sales-gated "Get a demo" in a self-serve small-salon market.
7. Template sameness on the client booking page (SimplyBook complaint).
8. Claiming uplift stats (Phorest style) with no local proof.

## 8. Opportunities for an original, warm, Mediterranean, crafted identity
- **Type pairing:** a high-contrast or soft-wedge serif for display (e.g. Fraunces, Playfair-adjacent, or a Cypriot/Greek-friendly serif; verify Turkish glyphs İ ı ğ ş ç ö ü) + a humanist sans for UI (e.g. Instrument Sans / Figtree). Add a small tracked-caps label style for service names and times. Keep Turkish dotted-I correct in uppercase transforms.
- **Palette:** limewash off-white paper, terracotta, olive, deep sea-blue ink, saffron for primary action; avoid pure white and pure black. Salon-level accent override so each salon feels like itself (Phorest's branded-app insight, without copying it).
- **Texture:** subtle paper grain, hand-cut/stamped edges on cards, linocut or tile-pattern (Cypriot ceramics, Lefkara lace geometry) as section dividers; use sparingly.
- **Illustration vs photography:** photography of real Northern Cyprus shops (hands, tools, shop-front tiles, mirrors, light) as hero; illustration only for empty states and small spot icons (scissors, comb, razor, bergamot) in a single hand-drawn line style.
- **Calendar:** appointments as "tickets" or appointment-card shapes with a barber-pole or tile colour code per stylist; warm-neutral grid, no harsh gridlines; a "today" thread line like a sewing thread.
- **Booking flow:** 3 steps, large tappable service cards with duration and price in the serif; stylist choice with a portrait and "first available" default; WhatsApp confirmation and a TR/EN toggle visible from step 1; mobile-first (most Northern Cyprus clients book by phone/WhatsApp [I]).
- **Motion:** slow 400-600 ms ease-out reveals, a gently drawn underline/stitch on selection, a tile "flip" when a slot is confirmed; honour `prefers-reduced-motion`; no parallax carousels.
- **Trust, locally:** real salon names/photos, "booked via WhatsApp" proof, plain pricing in TRY/GBP/EUR, owner-voiced copy in Turkish first.
- **Premium signal:** fewer, bigger things; editorial captions; restrained colour; real craft details beat feature grids.
